Sustainable

The apron itself is made from a 100% sustainable material called TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane).

It is a very flexible and smooth material, but at the same time extremely strong and durable.

TPU is safe, 100% non-toxic, recyclable and biodegradable and is a much healthier alternative to PVC which contains toxic chemicals, is harmful to the environment and is very expensive to recycle. TPU offers high wear resistance and possesses excellent resistance to UV, oils, greases, and solvents. This is particularly important when considering this is an apron which must be able to withstand a little bit of everything.
